Everyday we get younger

I'm James, a 22 year old from Texas. I am an eclectic thinker, researcher, and design engineer. I am driven by a passion to combine great aesthetics with technical expertise. My design practice is fueled by principles of phenomenology, a multidisciplinary study of human experience that explores how perception shapes good design.

I enjoy exploring different mediums of art like music, film, and fashion through different side projects. Alongside that, a passion for learning shapes how I spend my time through reading, writing, discussion, and a consistent meditation practice.

“My motivation is to fulfil any potential I have to contribute beauty to the world before I die.”
